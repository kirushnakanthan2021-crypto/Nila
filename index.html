What this version can do

  • Keep chat history on this device
  • Save notes and retrieve related notes in chat
  • Export and restore your notes
  • Work offline after the first load, where supported
  • Optionally connect to the Python/SQLite retrieval backend

This is still a retrieval-based foundation, not a full language model or autonomous learner. No external AI model is connected.
